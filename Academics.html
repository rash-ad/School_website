Academics at Al-Mubarak National School, Malwana

At Al-Mubarak National School, Malwana, we believe that education is more than passing exams — it’s about developing knowledge, discipline, creativity, and character. Our academic programs are carefully designed to meet national standards while encouraging curiosity, innovation, and independent thinking in every student.

Grade Levels

We offer education from Grade 6 to Grade 13, catering to students in the junior secondary, senior secondary, and advanced level stages. Junior Secondary (Grades 6–9): Students build a strong foundation in key subjects such as Mathematics, Science, English, Sinhala, Tamil, History, Geography, and ICT. The focus is on developing study habits, communication skills, and critical thinking. Senior Secondary (O/L Section – Grades 10–11): Students prepare for the G.C.E. Ordinary Level Examination, following the national curriculum. Along with compulsory subjects, they can choose from various subject baskets that match their interests and goals.

Advanced Level (A/L Section – Grades 12–13):

  • Science Stream – For students interested in medicine, engineering, and technology.
  • Commerce Stream – For future entrepreneurs, accountants, and business leaders.
  • Arts Stream – For creative, analytical, and language-oriented students.
  • Technology Stream – A modern path focusing on applied sciences, IT, and technical skills.

Modern Teaching & Learning

We integrate digital learning tools and smart classrooms to make lessons interactive and engaging. Teachers use innovative methods such as project-based learning, group discussions, and multimedia resources to enhance student understanding.

Laboratories & Resources

To support practical learning, our school features: Science and computer laboratories A state-of-the-art technical laboratory A well-stocked library Access to online learning materials These facilities help students apply theory to real-life practice — preparing them for both exams and future careers.

Academic Achievements

Over the years, Al-Mubarak students have consistently performed well in O/L and A/L examinations, earning recognition for their academic excellence and discipline. Our alumni have gone on to succeed in various professional fields both locally and internationally.
